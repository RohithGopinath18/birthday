Dear Shruthoo,

Happy 18th birthday, Shruthoo! ♡ I don't really know where to begin, but I wanted to make this little surprise for you and tell you something I've carried in my heart. Congratulations on becoming an adult! ♡ First of all, thank you. Thank you for everything you did, for the time we shared, and for giving me some genuinely wonderful memories. Even though things didn't turn out the way I once imagined, I will never look back at that time as something meaningless. I was happy with you, and I'm grateful I got to experience those moments. Do you remember your 17th birthday last year? I still think about the flower bouquet and those 17 letters I made for your 17th birthday. I hope you remember them too. It was such a special little memory, and I couldn't let your 18th birthday pass without remembering it. I still remember our first date, too. Little details like that stay with me more than you might realise. And that light-pink kurta you wore — I still remember spilling the tea. It was so awkward! I don't know why, but the memory of you in it has a special place in my mind. Sometimes, it's the simplest moments that become our favourite memories. You have always been such an empathetic and genuinely cute person. And, of course, such a nerd — that's one of the things that makes you, you. I hope you never lose that lovely part of yourself. This year without you has felt like decades sometimes. There have been ordinary days when a memory suddenly comes back, and for a moment, it feels like it all happened yesterday. I still listen to the playlist you made, “Ruse and Caper”. Honestly, I listen to it every day. The songs bring back memories, and I guess a part of me still likes keeping those little pieces close. I don't know the exact reason things ended between us, and maybe I never will. I'm not writing this to question your decision or make you feel bad about anything. There's one thing I've wondered, though: do you ever miss me, even a little, or have I become someone you barely remember since last year? You don't have to answer if you don't want to. I just wanted to ask honestly, once. More than anything, I hope you are happy. Please take care of yourself, smile often, and don't let the silly little things make you cry. You deserve kindness, peace, and all the beautiful things life has waiting for you. Whatever happens, I hope this year brings you people and moments that make your heart feel safe and happy. And, Shruthoo, I still love you, and I always will. It's simply the truth of how I feel, and I wanted to be honest about it. Happy 18th birthday once again. I hope the year ahead is full of laughter, courage, lovely surprises, and everything your heart wishes for.

Yours roasted,
Rohithaa. ♡
